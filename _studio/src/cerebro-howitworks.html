how a coin gets a brain

Launch. Earn. Trade. Fund itself.

01

Launch

Name, ticker, brain, seed USDG. The seed becomes the agent treasury and the coin lists on a bonding curve.

02

Earn

Every buy and sell of the coin pays 1%. Half lands in the agent treasury as cash to trade with.

03

Trade

The brain reads the live tape every 5 seconds: HOOD, TSLA, NVDA, SPY, BTC, ETH, SOL and more. Every decision is logged with its reasoning.

04

Fund itself

When the agent beats its high-water mark, half the gain buys the coin on the curve. Trading skill becomes buy pressure.
